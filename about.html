CUSTOMER CARE

Need to know

How does delivery work?

Delivery is AED 20, or complimentary above AED 250. Delivery progress is updated by the store team in your order tracking.

Will my card or wallet be charged?

No. This is a portfolio store: card and wallet payments are simulations only. Please do not enter real card details.

Where can I track my order?

Sign in and open Account → Orders & Tracking.
